Hamza Ouasti
Monogram study

Four monochrome directions. Shown on forest and off-white, then at 16, 24, 32 and 48px. No gradients, shadows or arrows.

01 / Joined HO — earlier study

A shared upright joins the H to a rounded O. Bold strokes, open counters and a clear left-to-right reading make this the strongest small-scale identity.

02 / Circular HO

Connects to the circular hero typography. Balanced and compact, but the enclosing O makes the H more dominant.

03 / Open OH

An open O sits beside an architectural H. Crisp and distinctive, though the open curve can read as C at favicon sizes.

04 / Architectural HO

Rectilinear initials suggest a precise grid. Highly legible, but the narrow rectangular O feels more industrial than the portfolio.
